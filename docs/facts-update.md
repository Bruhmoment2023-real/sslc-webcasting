## 3. The 2024 before-grace pass figure (about 54%)

**No primary table exists.** Status: **[N]** from at least three outlets, and it passes an arithmetic cross-check.

- The board's press note says the pass percentage "has declined by 30% over last year" and that after normalisation it is 73.40% **[P]**. 83.89 - 30 = 53.89, so "declined by 30%" is consistent with about 54% if it means percentage points; the note does not print the figure.
- Deccan Herald, 9 May 2024 (pack copy): about 54% before grace. Deccan Herald editorial: "from 83.89% to a shocking 54%" (search summary).
- Careers360, 10 Oct 2024 (opened): "SSLC results showed a pass rate of 54%, which rose to 74% with the addition of 20% grace marks."
- Udayavani, May 2024 (search summary only): 73.40%, "a significant decrease of 10.49%"; Deccan Herald reports of "about 1.70 lakh" students helped (one Deccan Herald piece says "over 1.6 lakh").
- My arithmetic: 631,204 passes minus 54% of 859,967 appeared (about 464,400) gives about 166,800, close to "1.7 lakh" - the two figures are mutually consistent but both come from the same reporting.
- Nobody printed 53.9 or 53.89; I did not find that number anywhere.
- I found no Karnataka Legislative Assembly or Council record with the figure (section 6).
